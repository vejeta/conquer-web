Your first turn

A real first turn of a new nation, key by key: sign in, read the reports, march out to take land, draft troops, and hand in your orders. Every screen below comes from the game itself.

The recording above is the real terminal: pause it at any moment, and select and copy its text.

  4. 04 Read the main screen

    just look

    This is where you spend the game. It has four parts:

    • The map, left. C is your capital. Letters in reverse video are sectors you own. The others show the land: ~ water, - flat, % hills, ^ mountains, # peaks.
    • Your units, top right. * marks the selected one, > the others in the same sector. mv is how far each one can still move this turn, st its orders.
    • The sector under the cursor, bottom right: owner, what it is used for, vegetation, terrain, people.
    • The status line, bottom: turn, treasury in gold talons, season, and You have Conquer Mail when there are messages.

    You start with only your capital. Everything else has to be taken.

    B

  15. 15 Save and quit

    Spaceq

    Press q. Your orders are saved, and the menu asks whether you have finished this turn.

      Are your orders for this turn done? [y/N]

  16. 16 Mark your orders done

    y

    Answer y. When every nation has done the same, the turn update runs straight away; otherwise it runs at the scheduled time. You can come back and change your orders until then.

      Are your orders for this turn done? [y/N]
  Orders marked as done. You can still change them until the update.

  Every nation is ready: the turn update starts now.
  Come back in a few minutes for the new turn.

Four rules that save your first year

Big enough to conquer. An army takes empty land only with at least 75 soldiers and one for every 350 of your people. Send your largest field army.

Drafting stops an army. Troops added to an army use up its movement for the turn. Raise them as a new army.

Food first. A capital alone grows no food. Take flat land with good vegetation and turn it into farms (r on a sector you own).

Quit when you are done. The turn cannot run while you are in the game. Press q and mark your orders done.

Practise without risk

Option 7 in the game menu opens your own practice world: the same game, with nobody else in it. Try anything, run the next turn yourself and start again whenever you like. The coach (Coach button above the game) walks you through this same turn inside the terminal.
