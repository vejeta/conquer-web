Green phosphor over the mountains

The design system for Conquer: a green monitor from a 1987 computing centre, set under the mountains and pine forests of Middle-earth. This page is a sample of every component, not part of the site.

Race

Each race tints the phosphor, the surfaces, stone and gold. Nothing else moves. Open with #race=elf.

Colour

The phosphor is the only accent (green for Humans). Gold marks scores, ranks and founded nations, never buttons. Brick red appears only when something is wrong.
