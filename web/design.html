Green phosphor over the mountains

The design system for Conquer: a green monitor from a 1987 computing centre, set under the mountains and pine forests of Middle-earth. This page is a sample of every component, not part of the site.

Race

Each race tints the phosphor, the surfaces, stone and gold. Nothing else moves. Open with #race=elf.

The game screen

Each race looks at the world through its own architecture: white-stone Gondor with its kings, silver Rivendell with swans, the dwarf hall with its hammer, the black gate of Mordor. The 80 by 24 terminal inside is untouched.

The frame is architecture, sized by the width of its own block, not the window: columns 72 / 44 / 6 px, lintel 88 / 56 / 36 px, sill 56 / 36 / 18 px (large, medium, phone). Corner sculptures appear from 700 px. Hover or focus the Dwarf terminal to light the runes; click the Elf terminal to ripple the water.

  


  


  

    
The gate

    
The Doors of Durin, closed: the future login dialog, the same for every race. The archway is the dwarf frame: lintel with the crown of seven stars, anvil-capped pillars, axe-heads above and guardians below.

Colour

    

    
The phosphor is the only accent (green for Humans). Gold marks scores, ranks and founded nations, never buttons. Brick red appears only when something is wrong.
