Create your website account

Playing Conquer takes two keys. This page makes the first one.

  1. 1. Your account for this website (here, now) A name and a password that open the door of the site. Your browser asks for them in a small window each time you press Play. It is not your nation: other players never see it.
  2. 2. Your nation (next, inside the game) Conquer is a game from 1987 that runs in a terminal. After you sign in, it asks you to found your nation: its name (up to 9 letters, it can differ from your account), race, class, and a nation password the game asks for every time you play.

First key ready: your account for this website

  1. Press Play now. Your browser shows a small sign-in window: type and the password you just chose.
  2. If the browser does not ask and signs you in as someone else (it remembers the last account used on this site), open this site in a private window.
  3. Second key: the game asks you to found your nation. Choose its name (up to 9 letters), race and class, and a nation password. Save at the end with Esc and then y.
  4. From then on, Play now and your account take you straight to your nation, which asks for its password.
