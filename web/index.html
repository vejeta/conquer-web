What is Conquer?

Conquer is a turn-based fantasy strategy game for many players, first released on Usenet in 1987 by Ed Barlow and Adam Bryant. Each player rules a nation: build cities, raise armies, cast magic, trade and negotiate with other rulers, and expand across a shared world.

How to join

The game is in a test phase: nations are created by the game administrator.

  1. Ask the administrator for a nation. You will get a nation name and its password, plus the site access credentials.
  2. Press Play now and sign in with the site access credentials.
  3. In the menu choose 1 Play, then type your nation name and nation password.

How turns work

  • Log in as often as you like to give orders: move armies, draft troops, build, trade, send messages.
  • All orders are resolved together at the next turn update. During the test phase updates are weekly; the in-game menu shows the exact schedule and the last update.
  • The update waits while players are connected, so press q to quit the game when you are done.

Before you play

  • Use a computer with a keyboard. Conquer is controlled entirely with keys.
  • The game needs a terminal of at least 80×24 characters. If the menu warns that the screen is too small, enlarge the window or zoom out with Ctrl + -.
  • If the screen looks garbled, press Ctrl + L to redraw it.
