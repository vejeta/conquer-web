4. Legal bases (GDPR, Article 6)

  • Contract or steps before a contract (6.1.b): your account and your nation, so that you can play.
  • Legitimate interest (6.1.f): server logs and abuse control, to keep a small public service safe and running.
  • Consent (6.1.a): the optional email and message. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

6. Public game data

The game is public by nature. The Hall and the status page show nation names, race, class, alignment, scores and news. They never show account names, emails or invite codes. Other players can see your nation's public actions and can send you in-game messages.

7. International transfers

The servers are in {{HOSTING_COUNTRY}}. If a provider is outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is covered by the safeguards of Chapter V of the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses. Backups stay in Spain.

8. How long data is kept

  • Pending sign-up requests (email, message): up to 30 days; they are removed as soon as the administrator decides.
  • Server logs (IP address, user agent): 14 days.
  • Backups: 30 days. A deletion is therefore complete in our backups within 30 days.
  • Accounts and game data: until you ask us to delete them, or until the end of a season (the world is then reset) and you ask.

9. Cookies and local storage

We only use what the service needs. For that reason there is no cookie banner: strictly necessary cookies need no consent (LSSI-CE, article 22.2).

  • conquer_session (cookie, HttpOnly, Secure): keeps you signed in. It lasts until you close the browser, or 30 days if you tick "Remember me". It contains your account and password in encrypted form, readable only by our server. Sign out to remove it.
  • Local storage and session storage in your browser: your language, sound and display settings, tutorial progress, and the practice world. They are never sent to us. Clear them in your browser settings at any time.

10. Security

The site uses HTTPS. Website passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Backups are encrypted. Only the administrator has access to the server.

A caveat about passwords: the game itself, written in 1987, keeps at most 7 characters of a nation password and hashes it with an old method that can be guessed offline by anyone who gets the game files. Do not reuse a password you use anywhere else.

11. Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data. Write to the email address in section 1 and tell us your account name. We answer within one month.

To delete your account: email us and say so. We remove the account, any pending request and your nation. Copies in backups disappear within 30 days.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (https://www.aepd.es).

12. The Android app

  • Permissions: INTERNET (to reach the game), VIBRATE, and POST_NOTIFICATIONS (to tell you that a turn has run).
  • The notification is created on your phone. The app checks the public status of the game about once an hour. There is no push service and no Google or Firebase token.
  • When you sign in, the app sends your account name and password to our server over HTTPS. It stores them on your phone, encrypted, only if you choose so. "Forget account" removes them. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking code of our own.

13. Children

Conquer is not aimed at children under 14, the age of digital consent in Spain. If you are under 14, do not sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 14, we delete it.

14. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we update the date at the top and say so on the sign-up page. Date of this version: 2026-11-04.
