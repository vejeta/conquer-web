Change your password

At least 8 characters, 72 at most.

After the change you are signed out: sign in again with the new password.

Recovery code

The recovery code is the only way to get your account back if you forget your password. Making a new one cancels the old one: save the new one.

Making a code needs your current password.

If you lose both your password and your recovery code, write to conquer@vejeta.com: accounts cannot be restored without the code. You can join again next season.
